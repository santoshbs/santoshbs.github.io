Hypothesis: In the mid term, the biological validation of relational care and the deployment of clinical AI may lead to a formal reconfiguration of treatment planning and care coordination.

Far (2046–2051): For registered nurses broadly, the structural evolution of the role depends on whether AI-enabled documentation and biological evidence for relational care create space for a presence-based practice or if systemic labor shortages prioritize technical output [6][3]. In one scenario, the task of coordinating with healthcare team members may shift toward a model where biological markers of patient well-being are used to audit the quality of nursing presence [3]. Hypothesis A: In the far term, nursing may bifurcate into a highly technical AI-managed layer and a specialized “presence” layer validated by biological markers of patient well-being.
