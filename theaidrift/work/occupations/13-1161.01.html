Mid (2036–2041): For search marketing strategists broadly, if generative AI interfaces become the primary discovery mechanism for most users, the task of combining secondary data sources with keyword research to profile user intent may shift toward optimizing the data contexts used in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) [1][2]. This would require strategists to manage a brand’s presence within the specific datasets and knowledge graphs that AI models prioritize for factual retrieval [1]. Extrapolating from the rise of AI agents, the task of conducting online marketing initiatives may shift from driving traffic to digital storefronts toward optimizing the agent-facing metadata that determines which product an AI agent selects for a user [4]. However, the task of coordinating with developers to optimize website architecture persists because structured data remains the primary technical mechanism for AI models to ingest verified, authoritative information [2].

Hypothesis: In the mid term, the occupation may shift toward managing brand “knowledge footprints” and agent-facing metadata to ensure favorable selection by generative AI models.

Far (2046–2051): For search marketing strategists broadly, the occupation may diverge based on the evolution of algorithmic transparency. One plausible outcome is a specialized role focused on brand sentiment and identity curation, ensuring that human-centric brand affinity translates into AI visibility [1], while continuing the task of coordinating sales or other promotional strategies. A rival outcome involves the near-total automation of visibility management, where the strategist focuses on high-level creative inputs while agents manage the execution of visibility [11]. The degree to which platforms allow “influence” over AI responses remains a primary unknown. The task of coordinating sales or other promotional strategies with merchandising and inventory staff will likely resist automation because it depends on physical supply chain constraints and internal organizational incentives.

Hypothesis A: In the far term, the occupation may evolve into a brand identity curation role that ensures human brand affinity is effectively encoded for AI discovery.

Hypothesis B (competing): In the far term, the tactical elements of the occupation may be fully subsumed by automated agentic systems, leaving strategists to focus exclusively on high-level creative and strategic inputs.
