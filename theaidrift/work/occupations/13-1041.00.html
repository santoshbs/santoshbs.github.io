Human judgment remains the primary mechanism for resolving contradictions between automated flags and legal nuance.

Hypothesis: In the mid term, automated monitoring tools may shift the officer’s role from evidence collection to the verification of algorithmic flags, though interpretive advisory tasks remain human-led.

Far (2046–2051): For compliance officers in digitally-integrated settings, outcomes depend on whether regulatory standards are codified into machine-readable formats [3]. One scenario involves the total automation of verifying that regulatory policies have been implemented, shifting the role toward the oversight of compliance system architecture [3].
