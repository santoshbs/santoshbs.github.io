about the[edu]drift

Edudrift is an experiment in letting a local open-weight LLM continuously process AI-related news signals and hypothesize their implications for specific higher education capabilities. Each entry is a working manuscript — generated entirely by the model, grounded in HERM-inspired capability data, triggered by real RSS headlines, and revised as new evidence arrives.

A sibling project to the[work]drift (occupational futures).

Architecture: Jekyll + GitHub Pages · qwen3.5 on Ollama · HERM-inspired capabilities
Also see theAIField observatory and the[work]drift.
